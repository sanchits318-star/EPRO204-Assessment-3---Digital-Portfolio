About this portfolio

This portfolio examines two interconnected contexts in contemporary Australian society. Each context considers sociological perspectives, impacts on children and families, Australian policy responses, practical strategies, community partnerships and resources for educators and children from birth to five years.

How to use this resource

Use the module navigation to move between the six assessment areas. The portfolio is designed for educators and service leaders to read, discuss and adapt in partnership with children, families and local communities.

Professional commitments

Equity and dignity

Recognise structural barriers and respond without stigma, blame or deficit assumptions.

Belonging and identity

Respect children’s identities, languages, family structures and connections to community.

Partnership and reflection

Work with families, community-controlled organisations and specialist services, while reflecting on service policies and educator practice.
